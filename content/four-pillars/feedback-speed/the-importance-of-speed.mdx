Fast feedback limits how much work you may need to redo. Suppose you are adding
a page where users can assign attribute-based permissions. You need to:

- Add all required form fields.
- Validate and format the field values.
- Define a suitable structure for the form data.
- Connect the form to the backend API.

You estimate that the page will take one week to finish:

If you collect **feedback\*** only at the end of the week, a bug from day two
may affect everything built after it. Fixing it could require changes across
several components and their logic.

Shorter feedback cycles catch bugs and misunderstood requirements before more
work depends on them:

<img
  src={'/images/four-pillars/iterative-changes.png'}
  alt={

_**Feedback** here answers two questions: Did we build the right thing? Did we
build it without bugs?_

If each cycle takes about a day, you can catch problems within a day instead of
at the end of the week. But an early mistake can still waste that day. Next,
we'll shorten the cycle further.
