This is a self-paced course, and all content is available from the get-go. We
recommend spending at least 10 minutes per day on each lesson to keep you
motivated.

Moreover, don't underestimate the value of reviewing the content of the previous
sections and **doing the exercises**. One of the most effective ways of learning
is using [Retrieval Practice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Testing_effect),
which is a fancy term to describe the process of "trying to remember something
you've just learned only using your memory, without access to the original
material". That is even more effective than active learning practices like
mind-map modeling, so you should give it a try.
