The goal of this mini-course is to teach you the tools and knowledge necessary
to evaluate the properties of automated tests and be able to judge on their
quality.

Through quizzes and lessons, we'll cover the following topics:

1. **What is a unit.**
2. **What are good and bad automated tests.**
3. **How to evaluate tests' maintainability.**
4. **How to assess test feedback speed.**
5. **How to determine how much a test protects your system against
   regressions.**
6. **The Trilema of automated tests.**

The contents of this course are highly inspired by Vladimir Khorikov's book
entitled
_[Unit Testing Principles, Practices and Patterns](https://www.amazon.de/-/en/Unit-Testing-Principles-Practices-Patterns/dp/1617296279)_.
We recommend reading it if you'd like to deep dive into this topic.
